# **App Name**: Rare Zanzibar Adventure

## Core Features:

- AI Adventure Concierge: An intelligent travel tool that uses reasoning to generate bespoke Zanzibar and safari itineraries based on traveler preferences and seasonal conditions.
- Cinematic Immersive Hero: Fullscreen video backgrounds featuring autoplay Zanzibar beach and safari footage with Framer Motion fade-in text.
- Luxury Tour Showcase: Interactive, hover-animated cards for Stone Town, Safari, and Water Sports experiences with deep-zoom image effects.
- Scroll-Triggered Growth Stats: Dynamic, animated counters showcasing completed tours and happy traveler metrics as users explore the landing page.
- Glassmorphism Booking Desk: A frosted-glass contact interface featuring animated inputs, tour interest dropdowns, and integrated WhatsApp quick-launch links.
- Adventure Masonry Gallery: A responsive lightbox gallery utilizing lazy-loading to display high-resolution imagery of Tanzanian wildlife and coastal scenery.
- Social Wave Footer: A high-end animated footer with custom SVG ocean wave transitions and structured regional contact information.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Deep Ocean Azure (#296E85), a sophisticated blue reflecting the depth of the Zanzibar sea.
- Background Color: Midnight Abyss (#0F1213), a very dark, desaturated navy providing a premium high-contrast canvas.
- Accent Color: Tropical Mint (#26D9B0), a vibrant and refreshing analogous green for buttons and active highlights.
- Font pairing: 'Playfair' (serif) for elegant, high-contrast headlines and 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for warm, legible body text.
- Wildlife and ocean-inspired line icons from 'React Icons', styled with gradient fills for a modern aesthetic.
- Mobile-first, masonry-grid based sections with spacious vertical padding to evoke the openness of the island landscape.
- Parallax scrolling combined with Framer Motion spring physics for smooth, luxurious page transitions.
